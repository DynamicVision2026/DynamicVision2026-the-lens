## Pass lines (founder)

| Check | Pass if | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sample size | **≥ 30 answers** with Q0 = 小学1年〜中学3年 | Exclude "その他". Below 30, keep collecting; do not decide. |
| Anchor pain | **≥ 40% answer 毎回 + よくある** on Q1 | Of the valid answers above. Both conditions must hold. |

Secondary readouts (inform, do not gate):
- Q1 split by grade band (小1–3 / 小4–6 / 中学) — tells which grade to launch first.
- Q4 "続かなかった + 時間がない" share — size of the "already believes in it" segment.
- Q6 share choosing ¥500 or more, and Q7 email count — early signal for pricing and the waitlist.
- Q5 phrases — reuse verbatim in copy (no score claims, even if parents write them).
